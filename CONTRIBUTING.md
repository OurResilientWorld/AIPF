# Contributing to AI Product Flow

Contributions should make the guides and case studies more accurate, useful, testable or accessible.

## Choose the right route

- **Correction:** identify an exact passage, defect and proposed correction.
- **New evidence:** provide a public source that may strengthen, weaken or qualify a finding.
- **Improvement proposal:** propose clearer guidance, a useful case study or a practical test of a gap.
- **Accessibility:** report a barrier in navigation, reading order, contrast or alternatives.

Open the relevant GitHub issue form in this repository. A GitHub account is required. Include the page URL, edition and exact passage where relevant. For evidence, include its source, locator, date, context and limitations.

Issues and comments are public. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information, personal information about other people, credentials or material you do not have permission to share.

## Review and decisions

Triage classifies a submission; it does not accept evidence or automatically change published guidance. Maintainers review the issue, ask for clarification where needed, and record the decision in the issue. Changes are prepared privately and enter a published edition only after review and Product Owner approval. There is no promised response time.

Be respectful and evidence-focused. Do not harass, discriminate, dox, threaten or misrepresent another contributor's evidence. Maintainers may close, hide or report material that breaches these rules or GitHub's policies.

This follows the contribution approach of [AI-Assisted Research](https://github.com/OurResilientWorld/AI-Assisted-Research/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), adapted to AIPF guides and case studies.
