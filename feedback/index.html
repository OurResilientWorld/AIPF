Contribute to AI Product Flow

Help make the guides and case studies more accurate, useful, testable or accessible.

Choose the right route

GitHub issues and comments are public. Do not include confidential, sensitive or personal information. A GitHub account is required to submit.

  • Suggest a correction
  • Share new evidence
  • Propose an improvement
  • Report an accessibility barrier

Each route explains the questions before you open its GitHub form. Enter your response on GitHub; this website does not collect or send it.

What happens next?

Maintainers classify the issue, review it and may ask for clarification. Submission does not automatically change a guide, accept evidence or commit to an improvement. Accepted changes are prepared privately and released after review and Product Owner approval. No response time is promised.
