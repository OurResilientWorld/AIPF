Contribute to AI Product Flow

Help make the guides and case studies more accurate, useful, testable or accessible.

GitHub account and sign-in required

You will write and submit your feedback on GitHub. Sign in when prompted, or create a GitHub account before continuing.

Feedback and comments are public. Do not include confidential, sensitive or personal information.

Choose the right route

  • Correction
  • New evidence
  • Improvement proposal
  • Accessibility

Each route links to its GitHub form. You enter your answers only on GitHub.

What happens next?

After you submit, GitHub creates a public issue with its own page. You can return to it to read replies and continue the discussion.

Maintainers classify the issue, review it and may ask for clarification. Submission does not automatically change a guide, accept evidence or commit to an improvement. Accepted changes are prepared privately and released after review and Product Owner approval. No response time is promised.
